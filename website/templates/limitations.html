Limitations and intended use

  • One WQP result profile; vendor formats and other portals are out of scope.
  • No multi-limit linkage, no inference of missing units, fractions, methods or qualifier meanings.
  • Reporting-limit bounds for DL records may be looser than the unavailable MDL; downstream summaries inherit that uncertainty.
  • Five held-out exports are not a probability sample; records are clustered within sites.
  • Interpretation labels and the same-specification baseline are AI-assisted; there is no human expert validation.
  • No estimator, policy ranking, regulatory determination or clinical authority.
  • The living website is a demo, not evidence of adoption, usability or performance.
